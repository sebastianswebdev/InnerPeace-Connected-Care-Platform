Overview

Compassionate clinical support in the community

Working alongside individuals, families, carers, and providers, we deliver person-centred dementia support that promotes dignity, safety, wellbeing, and meaningful connection.

Service Area

Families and carers

Home care recipients

Aged care and community providers

How We Help

Practical guidance with a human centre

Our support is designed to strengthen care, reduce distress, and help people remain connected to identity, independence, and quality of life.

  • Personalised care planning
  • Family and carer guidance
  • Changed behaviour support and dementia strategies
  • Support for home care and aged care providers
  • Improving wellbeing, independence and quality of life

Specialist Clinical Services

Specialist dementia care, translated into practical action

Clinical insight is paired with clear, usable strategies for individuals, families, carers, and care teams.

1

Behaviour Assessment & Support

Comprehensive assessment of behaviour with practical strategies to reduce distress and improve care.

2

Individualised Care Planning

Development and review of person-centred care plans responsive to changing needs.

3

Family & Carer Support

Education, guidance and practical tools for families and informal carers.

4

Provider & Workforce Consultation

Clinical consultation for home care and aged care providers, and support teams to strengthen capability and consistency of care.

5

Quality of Life Strategies

Support focused on meaningful engagement, wellbeing and independence while maintaining identity and purpose.

6

Assessment, Diagnosis & Ongoing Support

Initial cognitive assessments, geriatric assessment, diagnosis support, and ongoing diagnostic support.
